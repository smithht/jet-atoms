Jet, Atoms, and Everything in Between

Harry Smith, 2026 · interactive real-time 3D, game controller

The exhibit running: one continuous zoom from the engine to the atoms. Silent clip.

An interactive journey through a jet engine, from the whole machine down to the atoms of one 3D-printed part. It moves continuously across more than nine orders of magnitude, joining design models, measured crystal data and computer simulation into a single view. The work asks whether AI tools can help researchers make their science visible, and worth caring about, to people outside their field, while staying honest about what is measured and what is drawn.

What is real: the crystals in the sample cube are a 3D X-ray scan of a real 3D-printed nickel-alloy part; the stress is a simulation on those crystals; the rest is designed or illustrated from published values. The colored bar on screen says which.
